# Academic Database Selection Guide

## Purpose

A field-specific reference for selecting the right databases for systematic literature searches. Use this to ensure your search is comprehensive and appropriate for the discipline.

---

## General-Purpose Databases (Search All Disciplines)

### Scopus
- **Coverage**: 22,000+ peer-reviewed journals; sciences, social sciences, humanities, arts
- **Strengths**: Citation analysis; author disambiguation; comprehensive coverage from ~1996
- **Best for**: Cross-disciplinary searches; citation tracking; impact metrics
- **Access**: Subscription required; many universities subscribe

### Web of Science (WoS)
- **Coverage**: Highly selective; ~21,000 journals; Science Citation Index, Social Sciences CI, Arts & Humanities CI
- **Strengths**: Long historical coverage; citation indexes; highly curated
- **Best for**: High-impact literature; bibliometric analysis; cross-disciplinary
- **Access**: Subscription required

### Google Scholar
- **Coverage**: Broadest coverage; includes grey literature, preprints, theses, books
- **Strengths**: Free access; finds literature missed by subscription databases; good for citation chaining
- **Weaknesses**: Cannot be systematically searched; limited filtering; includes low-quality sources
- **Best for**: Supplementary searching; finding specific items; grey literature
- **Note**: Never use as sole database for systematic search

### JSTOR
- **Coverage**: Humanities, social sciences; strong in older literature
- **Strengths**: Full-text access; humanities depth; archival content
- **Best for**: Historical searches; humanities; older literature

## Health & Medicine

### PubMed / MEDLINE
- **Coverage**: 35M+ citations; biomedical and life sciences from 1946
- **Strengths**: Free access; MeSH controlled vocabulary; comprehensive biomedicine
- **Best for**: Clinical research; public health; biomedical sciences
- **Access**: Free at pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov
- **Special feature**: MeSH (Medical Subject Headings) — use these for precision

### Cochrane Library
- **Coverage**: Cochrane Reviews; CENTRAL (controlled trials registry); methods studies
- **Strengths**: Gold standard for systematic reviews and RCTs
- **Best for**: Systematic reviews; clinical effectiveness; evidence-based practice
- **Access**: Free for abstracts; subscription for full reviews in some countries

### EMBASE
- **Coverage**: 30M+ records; strong in European publications and pharmacology
- **Strengths**: Emtree controlled vocabulary; drug and device studies; conference abstracts
- **Best for**: Pharmacology; device studies; European literature
- **Access**: Subscription required

### CINAHL (Cumulative Index to Nursing and Allied Health Literature)
- **Coverage**: Nursing, allied health, consumer health
- **Strengths**: Specialized in nursing and allied health
- **Best for**: Nursing research; physiotherapy; occupational therapy; public health nursing

## Psychology & Education

### PsycINFO
- **Coverage**: Psychology; behavioral sciences; mental health; from 1806
- **Strengths**: Comprehensive psychology coverage; APA-published; thorough indexing
- **Best for**: Psychology; psychiatry; behavioral sciences; counseling
- **Access**: Subscription (through APA or EBSCO)

### ERIC (Education Resources Information Center)
- **Coverage**: Education research; from 1966; US focus with international content
- **Strengths**: Free access; covers K-12 through higher education; policy documents
- **Best for**: Education research; curriculum; policy; teacher training
- **Access**: Free at eric.ed.gov

### PsycARTICLES
- **Coverage**: Full-text APA journal articles
- **Strengths**: Direct full-text access to APA publications
- **Best for**: When you need APA journal full text

---

## Engineering & Computer Science

### IEEE Xplore
- **Coverage**: IEEE publications; electronics, electrical engineering, CS; from 1884
- **Strengths**: Comprehensive IEEE content; standards; conference proceedings
- **Best for**: Electrical engineering; electronics; applied CS; robotics
- **Access**: Subscription required; free abstracts

### ACM Digital Library
- **Coverage**: Association for Computing Machinery publications
- **Strengths**: Definitive source for CS and information technology
- **Best for**: Computer science; software engineering; human-computer interaction
- **Access**: Subscription; free access to older content

### arXiv
- **Coverage**: Preprints in physics, mathematics, CS, quantitative biology, statistics
- **Strengths**: Free; cutting-edge research before peer review; broad CS coverage
- **Weaknesses**: Not peer-reviewed — flag all arXiv sources as preprints
- **Best for**: Finding latest research; CS and AI/ML especially
- **Access**: Free at arxiv.org

## Social Sciences & Humanities

### Sociological Abstracts
- **Coverage**: Sociology and related disciplines
- **Best for**: Sociology; social policy; criminology; demography

### Communication Abstracts
- **Coverage**: Communication, journalism, media studies
- **Best for**: Media research; journalism studies; communication theory

### Historical Abstracts
- **Coverage**: World history (excluding US/Canada)
- **Best for**: Historical research outside North America

### MLA International Bibliography
- **Coverage**: Literature, language, linguistics, folklore
- **Best for**: Literary studies; linguistics; language acquisition

## Business & Economics

### SSRN (Social Science Research Network)
- **Coverage**: Working papers and preprints in social sciences, business, law, economics
- **Strengths**: Free; cutting-edge unpublished research; widely used in economics
- **Weaknesses**: Not peer-reviewed — flag as preprints
- **Access**: Free at ssrn.com

### EconLit
- **Coverage**: Economics; 800,000+ records; from 1886
- **Best for**: Economics research; financial economics; labor economics

### Business Source Complete (EBSCO)
- **Coverage**: Business, management, economics; 2,300+ full-text journals
- **Best for**: Business research; management; marketing; finance

## Grey Literature Sources

Grey literature = reports, government documents, theses, conference papers, policy briefs not published in peer-reviewed journals. Include when peer-reviewed literature is insufficient or when policy relevance is important.

### OpenDOAR (Directory of Open Access Repositories)
- Registry of open access repositories worldwide
- Use to find institutional repositories

### BASE (Bielefeld Academic Search Engine)
- 350M+ documents from 10,000+ sources
- Strong for open access and grey literature

### DART-Europe
- European research theses
- Access to full text for many

### ProQuest Dissertations & Theses
- Comprehensive thesis/dissertation database
- Subscription required; abstracts free

### WHOLIS / WHO IRIS
- World Health Organization institutional repository
- Essential for global health grey literature

### Government portals
- CDC, NIH, OECD, World Bank, UN agencies, national statistics offices
- Document type: reports, white papers, policy documents

## Database Selection by Research Type

| Research Type | Primary | Secondary | Grey Literature |
|---------------|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| Clinical effectiveness | Cochrane, PubMed | EMBASE, CINAHL | WHO IRIS, govt health depts |
| Mental health interventions | PsycINFO, PubMed | EMBASE, CINAHL | NAMI, policy reports |
| Education interventions | ERIC, PsycINFO | Scopus, JSTOR | Dept of Education, OECD |
| Technology / AI | IEEE, ACM, arXiv | Scopus, Web of Science | Technical reports, white papers |
| Business strategy | Business Source, EconLit | Scopus, SSRN | Consulting firm reports, industry |
| Social policy | Scopus, Web of Science | Sociological Abstracts | OECD, govt agencies, think tanks |
| Environmental science | Web of Science, Scopus | PubMed | IPCC reports, EPA, UNEP |
| Cross-disciplinary | Scopus, Web of Science | Domain databases | BASE, OpenDOAR |

## Minimum Database Requirements

| Mode | Minimum Databases |
|------|------------------|
| Full systematic search | 3 databases (≥2 from primary field) + Google Scholar |
| Quick search | 2 databases + Google Scholar |
| Update search | Same databases as original search + date filter |
| Cite-check | No database search required |

## Notes on Search String Adaptation

Different databases use different syntax. Adapt Boolean strings:

| Feature | PubMed | Scopus | Web of Science |
|---------|--------|--------|----------------|
| Phrase search | "phrase" | "phrase" | "phrase" |
| Truncation | term* | term* | term* |
| Wildcards | term? | term? | term? |
| Field codes | [tiab] = title/abstract | TITLE-ABS-KEY() | TI= TS= |
| MeSH/Thesaurus | [MeSH] | N/A | N/A |
| Proximity | term1[tiab] AND term2[tiab] | W/N operator | NEAR/N operator |
